Look through our recent projects and you will find timber almost everywhere: in structures,
cladding, floors and joinery. It is not a style choice. It is the material that most often does
several jobs at once.

## What timber does well

- **It is fast.** Prefabricated timber frames go up in weeks, not months, with a drier, quieter site.
- **It stores carbon** instead of emitting it, especially when it comes from certified forests nearby.
- **It feels warm**, to the touch and to the eye, which matters more than any spec sheet suggests.

## Where we are careful

Timber needs to be kept dry and allowed to breathe. We detail every junction so water runs away
from it, we keep cladding off the ground, and we choose species and treatments according to
exposure: larch and chestnut outside, oak and ash inside.

Done well, a timber house is not a compromise. It is one of the most comfortable, durable and
honest ways we know to build a home.
