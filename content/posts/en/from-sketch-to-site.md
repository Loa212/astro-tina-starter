The most common question in a first meeting is not about style or materials. It is: *how long
until we can start building?* Here is what the months before the site opens usually look like.

## 1. Brief and survey (2–4 weeks)

We visit the site, measure everything, and spend time understanding how you live today. The output
is a written brief and a measured survey that everyone agrees on.

## 2. Concept design (4–6 weeks)

Two or three different directions, as sketches and simple models. This is the moment to be bold:
changing a plan now costs an afternoon, changing it on site costs a lot more.

## 3. Developed design and permits (2–4 months)

One direction, developed in detail with the engineers, and the drawings needed for planning
permission. Timings here depend on the municipality, so we submit as early as we can.

## 4. Tender and contract (4–6 weeks)

We prepare a full description of the works and invite two or three builders to price it. You
choose with a clear comparison, not three numbers on three different scopes.

When the site finally opens, the hardest decisions have already been made, calmly and on paper.
That is the whole point.
